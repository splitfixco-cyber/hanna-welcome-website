Welcome!

Hanna’s Universe is more than a collection of books — it is a creative and faith-centered world designed to support children and families through learning, emotions, and spiritual growth. From devotionals and vocabulary books to coloring books and illustrated stories, each creation responds to a different need and stage of life. The universe continues to grow, expanding soon to include a book for parents, offering guidance beyond childhood.

Available in English, Spanish and Portuguese, Hanna’s Universe connects diverse families through stories that nurture faith, curiosity, and emotional awareness. Different formats. Different ages. One shared purpose: to gently accompany growth with intention and care.

Characters

The emotional cast of Hanna's Universe.

01

Hanna

01

Hanna

Hanna is expressive, emotionally open, and deeply curious. Her face carries fear, wonder, joy, hesitation, and quiet courage.

She represents bravery through vulnerability: moving forward even when something new feels overwhelming.

02

Dogui

02

Dogui

Dogui is warm, affectionate, and always ready to encourage. He moves toward comfort, reassurance, and emotional safety.

He represents emotional support in the face of fear: the voice that says, "It's okay. I'm here."

03

Michiko

03

Michiko

Michiko is thoughtful, serious, and observant. He values logic, clarity, structure, and the power of reason.

He represents critical thinking and rational truth, while learning that logic must coexist with empathy.

04

Sagu

04

Sagu

Sagu is gentle, observant, and quietly brave. He listens before acting and is often the first to try something new.

He represents curiosity and openness to learning, balancing emotional awareness with thoughtful reflection.

05

The Mysterious Egg

05

The Mysterious Egg

The egg has tiny legs, sometimes wears a scarf, and has not yet hatched.

It represents knowledge not yet born: potential, beginnings, and unseen growth.

06

The Mouse

06

The Mouse

The mouse is always hidden behind objects, tucked into corners, or barely visible, and never speaks.

It represents hidden curiosity and the quiet learning that happens for those willing to look closely.
