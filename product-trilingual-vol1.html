A Systematic Trilingual Workbook for Vocabulary Acquisition and Character Writing.

Hanna’s Trilingual World. Volume 1: Hanna at School is a technical educational resource designed for students aged 8 to 12. This workbook provides a disciplined framework for practicing vocabulary in English, Spanish, and Korean (Hangul), specifically tailored for the cognitive maturity of the upper elementary and middle-school levels.

This volume focuses on the academic environment, utilizing the protagonist Hanna to contextualize essential school-related terminology.

Professional Educational Features:

  • Fine Motor Skill Reinforcement: The integration of coloring activities for specific school objects, such as "Glue" (Pegamento / 풀), is utilized as a manual engagement tool to stabilize attention and anchor the visual recognition of the term.

  • Structured Calligraphy Practice: Each lesson includes a precise grid for the tracing and independent writing of Korean characters. This facilitates the mastery of Hangul stroke order, a critical skill for students at this developmental stage.

  • Trilingual Phonetic Alignment: Each entry is presented with its English and Spanish equivalents and the corresponding Korean romanization, allowing the student to establish correct grapheme-phoneme connections across three languages.

  • Contextual Categorization: By focusing on the school setting, the workbook helps students organize and retrieve vocabulary related to their immediate academic surroundings, improving functional language use.

  • Optimized Cognitive Load: The layout is designed to be clean and professional, preventing visual overstimulation and allowing for the deep concentration required for character memorization and writing.

Technical Specifications:

  • Target Demographic: Students aged 8-12 (Primary/Middle School).

  • Core Objective: Mastery of introductory trilingual vocabulary and Hangul penmanship.

  • Design Philosophy: A high-contrast, manual resource intended for supplemental academic practice.

Hanna’s Trilingual World is a specialized tool for educators and parents seeking a rigorous, manual supplement for language study. It is designed to transform passive observation into active, trilingual literacy.
