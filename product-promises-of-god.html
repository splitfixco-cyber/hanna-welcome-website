Promises of God: A Gentle Devotional of Love, Care, and Hope for Children and Families

Buy on Amazon

A gentle devotional for children—filled with God’s promises that speak directly to a child’s heart.

Promises of God is a comforting devotional created especially for children and families. It can be read during the day or at night, making it perfect for quiet moments, reflection, or bedtime routines.

Each devotion shares a simple and loving promise from God—reminding children that they are seen, protected, and deeply loved. The words are written to meet a child where they are, speaking gently to their heart and offering peace, hope, and reassurance.

This devotional is perfect for:

  • Children’s devotional time
  • Daytime reflection or bedtime reading
  • Family devotion moments
  • Helping children understand God’s love in a calm and meaningful way

Promises of God invites children to rest in God’s presence and discover promises that bring comfort, trust, and a sense of safety—whether read in the morning, during the day, or before going to sleep.

This book was written with care to nurture a child’s faith and gently remind them that God’s love is always near.

A beautiful devotional to read, reflect on, and treasure—day or night.
