101 Bible Facts for Kids: Volume 1

Comprar na Amazon

Does God know all the stars? Who gave the sea its limits? How could Noah build such a big boat?

Kids ages 4 to 9 ask about everything, and every question opens a door to talk about God at home. In this first volume, Hanna and her friends Sagu, Michiko and Dogui find 34 Bible answers about the sky and the sea, seeds and animals, Noah and the rainbow, and how God made each one of us.

Each Bible fact takes just a few minutes to read at bedtime and includes:

  • A curious question, just like the ones kids ask

  • A short story with Hanna and her friends

  • A Bible verse from the New Living Translation (NLT)

  • The truth of the day, easy to remember

  • A simple prayer to pray together

  • A question to talk about as a family

Every fact comes with its own full-color illustration. This kids devotional is for parents who want to end the day talking about God with their children and listening to what they think.

Perfect for:

  • Bedtime reading and family devotions

  • Sunday school and children's ministry

  • Homeschool and character lessons

  • Christmas, Easter, birthday or baptism gifts

This is Volume 1 of the Bible Facts with Hanna collection. Volumes 2 and 3 complete all 101 Bible facts.
