The Essence of Raising Children: Conscious Parenting, Habits, and Emotional Health to Shape the Heart of Our Children

$17.99
Buy on Amazon

The Essence of Raising Children presents parenting as a profound, conscious, and deeply purposeful calling.

In this thoughtful and inspiring book, Rosalina Rangel explores the essential role of adult presence, the formation of healthy habits, and emotional well-being in a child's development. Each chapter reveals the shaping power of daily life and shows how words, routines, boundaries, and acts of love help form a child's inner world.

With warmth, clarity, and insight, this book offers meaningful guidance for:

  • Strengthening the bond between adults and children.

  • Cultivating healthy habits that last a lifetime.

  • Supporting emotional growth with sensitivity.

  • Building family life with stability, connection, and purpose.

Its perspective honors the whole child and affirms the importance of nurturing body, mind, heart, and soul with wisdom, dedication, and love.

The book invites parents and educators to see daily life as a meaningful space of formation, closeness, and hope. Every shared moment in the home carries lasting value, and every act of steady presence helps strengthen a child's inner security. Parenting is presented here as a work of lasting significance, sustained by consistency, tenderness, and the wisdom of the adult who guides.

A valuable resource for parents and educators, this book honors the daily work of nurturing, guiding, and shaping the heart of a child.
