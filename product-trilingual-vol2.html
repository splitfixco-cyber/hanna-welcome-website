A Systematic Trilingual Workbook for Domestic Vocabulary and Character Writing.

Hanna’s Trilingual World. Volume 2: Hanna at Home is a technical educational resource designed for students aged 8 to 12. This workbook provides a disciplined framework for practicing vocabulary in English, Spanish, and Korean (Hangul), specifically contextualized within the household environment to facilitate daily observation and language recall.

This volume explores the home setting, allowing students to bridge the gap between abstract learning and their immediate reality through the illustrations of Hanna.

Professional Educational Features:

  • Pattern Recognition & Domestic Context: By engaging with common household objects—from "House" (Casa / 집) to kitchen and bedroom items—the student practices identifying their surroundings in three distinct linguistic structures.

  • Tactile Vocabulary Anchoring: The coloring activities are strategically used as a manual engagement tool to stabilize focus on the trilingual terms, aiding in long-term memory retention.

  • Advanced Penmanship Practice: Each page features a dedicated, structured grid for the systematic tracing and independent writing of Korean characters. This facilitates the mastery of Hangul stroke order, essential for students at this developmental stage.

  • Phonetic and Cross-Linguistic Alignment: Includes English and Spanish equivalents with accurate Korean romanization to ensure the learner develops correct grapheme-phoneme connections.

  • Cognitive Load Management: The layout is intentionally clean and professional, preventing visual overstimulation and promoting the deep concentration necessary for trilingual literacy.

Technical Specifications:

  • Target Demographic: Students aged 8-12 (Upper Elementary / Middle School).

  • Core Objective: Mastery of domestic trilingual vocabulary and Hangul calligraphy.

  • Format: High-contrast, manual resource for supplemental academic use.
