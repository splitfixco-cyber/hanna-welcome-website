Meet the Characters

The emotional cast of Hanna's Universe.

Every friend in Hanna's world carries a feeling children recognize — courage, comfort, reason, curiosity, and the quiet wonder of things still waiting to grow. Tap a card to turn it over.

01

Hanna

01

Hanna

The heart of the universe. Hanna is expressive, emotionally open, and deeply curious — her face carries fear, wonder, joy, hesitation, and quiet courage.

She represents bravery through vulnerability: courage means moving forward despite fear, not the absence of it.

02

Dogui

02

Dogui

Dogui is warm, affectionate, and always ready to encourage. He moves toward comfort, reassurance, and emotional safety.

He represents emotional support in the face of fear: the voice that says, "It's okay. I'm here."

03

Michiko

03

Michiko

Michiko is thoughtful, serious, and observant. He values logic, clarity, structure, and the power of reason.

He represents critical thinking and rational truth, while learning that logic must coexist with empathy.

04

Sagu

04

Sagu

Sagu is gentle, observant, and quietly brave — often the mediator between his friends, listening before he acts.

He represents curiosity and balance: growth happens when empathy and reason meet.

05

The Mysterious Egg

05

The Mysterious Egg

The egg has tiny legs, sometimes wears a scarf, and has not yet hatched.

It represents knowledge not yet born: potential, beginnings, and unseen growth.

06

The Mouse

06

The Mouse

The mouse is always hidden behind objects, tucked into corners, or barely visible, and never speaks.

It represents hidden curiosity: learning often happens in unexpected places, quietly and subtly.
