A gentle 21-day devotional to help children grow in faith, prayer, and kindness ✨

Hanna’s Universe is a heartwarming 21-day devotional for kids designed to guide children through meaningful moments of faith, prayer, and friendship.

Each day invites children to:
✔ Enjoy short, faith-based stories they can easily understand
✔ Reflect on everyday situations through a biblical perspective
✔ Learn how to pray with honesty, trust, and confidence
✔ Grow values like kindness, gratitude, empathy, and faith in God

Perfect for:
• Kids ages 5–9
• Family devotional time
• Bedtime reading or quiet moments
• Parents, caregivers, teachers, and Sunday school leaders

With gentle language and comforting reflections, this devotional helps children discover that faith is not distant or complicated — it lives in their daily lives, emotions, and friendships.

A beautiful companion for growing hearts, curious minds, and families who want to nurture faith together.
